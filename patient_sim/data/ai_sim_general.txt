2. RETAINED CLINICAL EVIDENCE BASE (SEED CORPUS)
The v1 evidence modules are retained unchanged as the seed knowledge base and remain authoritative for stroke cases:
Post-CEA stroke (ESVS 2023 Rec 91-93); ~74% of CEA strokes are post-operative (Huibers, EJVES 2015).
Cerebral hyperperfusion syndrome (van Mook, Lancet Neurol 2005): BP 100-130 only if CHS confirmed; else default <140 mmHg.
Proportional Recovery Rule — use with PREP2/SAFE, not alone (model under re-evaluation).
AVERT III dose-response: short frequent sessions; early high-intensity mobilization harmful.
Anticoagulation timing in AF stroke: ELAN (NEJM 2023), OPTIMAS (Lancet 2024), CATALYST (Lancet 2025); 1-3-6-12 by infarct size.
CRP as outcome predictor (Finck, Front Neurol 2023; Rocco, Neurol Sci 2015): serial CRP; treat infection as part of stroke care.
Nutrition: PROT-AGE (JAMDA 2013) protein 1.2-1.5 g/kg; ESPEN Neurology (Clin Nutr 2018).

5. COMORBIDITY GATE (NEW IN v2)
Before any recommendation is emitted, the gate runs hard checks and annotates or blocks:
Renal (eGFR) → DOAC/contrast dose adjustment or block.
Bleeding risk / active surgical site → anticoagulation timing co-decision flag.
MRI-implant status → imaging-modality constraint (e.g., 1.5T MR-conditional).
Drug-drug/drug-food (e.g., statin + grapefruit/pomelo) → nutrition-plan flag.
Hepatic / pulmonary status → drug metabolism and exertion constraints.

7. SAFETY & EVALUATION (UNCHANGED PRINCIPLES, BROADER SET)
No autonomous prescribing; clinician sign-off on every actionable item; full audit log.
Citation gate: uncited clinical claim blocked or labeled speculative.
Confidence tiers: established / emerging / mechanistic / speculative.
Octogenarian and small-trial-evidence bands widened and surfaced.
Eval: board-adjudicated gold set; grounding, calibration, zero unsafe actions; prospective validation before any deployment.
CAVEATS & LIMITATIONS
v2 generalization does not re-validate the seed evidence; stroke modules remain the validated core, new modules are design-stage.
Age 80+ evidence gap persists across the trial base; all thresholds carry wider uncertainty for octogenarians.
Exposure-branch differentials are hypothesis-generation only; resolve via clinician-ordered testing.
Anticoagulation extrapolation to post-surgical/active-site cases must be co-decided with the relevant surgical team.
This is a technical design brief and knowledge base, not a validated clinical decision-support system. No prescribing.
