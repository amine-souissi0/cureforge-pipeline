2.1 Post-CEA Stroke (ESVS 2023 Recommendations 91-93)
Contemporary 30-day stroke rate after CEA in asymptomatic patients: 1.8% (meta-analysis cited in ESVS 2023). In the ICSS procedural-stroke substudy (Huibers et al., EJVES 2015), 20 of 27 CEA strokes (74.1%) occurred post-operatively rather than intra-operatively.
Source: Naylor R et al., Eur J Vasc Endovasc Surg 2023;65:7-111
2.2 Cerebral Hyperperfusion Syndrome (CHS)
Definition: ipsilateral headache, hypertension, seizures, and focal deficits after revascularization with cerebral blood flow increased >100% above baseline (van Mook et al., Lancet Neurol 2005;4:877-888).

2.7 CRP as Stroke Outcome Predictor
Finck et al., Front Neurol 2023 (n=676 large-vessel-occlusion thrombectomy patients): CRP >=5 mg/L associated with poor 90-day outcome (64.5% vs 42.1%; p<0.0001) and mortality (25.2% vs 9.4%). Youden optimal cut-offs: 3.5 mg/L for poor outcome; 8.5 mg/L for mortality.
Patient OIT CRP 14.6 mg/L is above both thresholds. Serial CRP at 48h and Day 7 is mandatory. UTI treatment is part of stroke management.
Rocco et al., Neurol Sci 2015 (n=1,242 thrombolysis cohort): follow-up CRP independently predicts 90-day dependent outcome (adj OR 2.67), mortality (adj OR 2.53), and sICH (adj OR 3.03). Trend matters more than single value.

CAVEATS AND LIMITATIONS
Age 80+ evidence gap: AVERT, ELAN, OPTIMAS, and decompressive-craniectomy trials under-recruit octogenarians. All recommendations carry a wider uncertainty band for this demographic. Shared decision-making with documented goals-of-care is mandatory.
PRR limitations: the proportional recovery rule is being re-evaluated as an explanatory model. Combine with PREP2/SAFE algorithm and clinical TMS/MEP data rather than using PRR alone for prognosis.
Citation correction: 'Rocco et al., Front Neurol 2023' in the case discussion is a citation merge. The Front Neurol 2023 CRP paper is Finck T et al. (PMID 37360331); the Rocco follow-up CRP paper is in Neurol Sci 2015;36:2235-2241. Both are retained correctly in Part 2.
ESVS 2023 Rec 91-93 evidence level: Class I confirmed; the precise evidence letter (likely C, from the Meershoek/de Borst 2021 Delphi) should be re-verified against Section 7.1.1 of the EJVES PDF before clinical-board sign-off.
ELAN/OPTIMAS/CATALYST did NOT specifically enroll post-CEA patients with active surgical sites. Extrapolation to Patient OIT is reasonable but must be co-decided with vascular surgery and documented.
Hyperperfusion BP target: 100-130 mmHg is the strict-control band for CHS-confirmed patients. Routine post-CEA target in the wider literature is <140/90 mmHg. The AI system should default to <140 mmHg unless hyperperfusion is confirmed or strongly suspected.
AI system is decision-support only -- no autonomous prescribing. Performance must be validated on a prospective, clinical-board-adjudicated test set before any patient-facing deployment. This document is a design brief, not a validated clinical tool.
